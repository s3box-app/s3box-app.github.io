Questions

What does it support?

Create and list buckets, upload, download, head, and delete objects, list with prefixes and folders, and presigned URLs. Requests are signed with SigV4. It does not do multipart upload, versioning, or ACLs yet.

Is it for production?

No. S3Box is for local development and testing, so you can build against S3 without a cloud account or a network.

Where are my files?

In a folder on your Mac. You choose the folder in the Server settings. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What does the app contact over the internet?

The license server, to check your key, and GitHub, to look for new versions. Your files and keys stay on your Mac.

What happens after the first year?

Your version keeps working. New versions need a renewed license.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel are both supported.
